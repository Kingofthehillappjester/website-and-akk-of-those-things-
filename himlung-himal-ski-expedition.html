The Expedition

The top of
the ladder.

Himlung sits in the Nar Phu valley close to the Tibetan border, a restricted area that sees a tiny fraction of Nepal’s visitors. At 7,126m it’s a serious expedition peak but not a technical one, which has made it a popular stepping stone towards the 8,000m peaks, and a good mountain to ski.

It also matters for anyone with Everest in mind: Nepal is moving to require a 7,000m Nepali summit before an Everest permit. This is a full expedition, with a base camp, three high camps, days spent waiting for weather and real cold. We keep the group small and the support high.

This is for you if…

  • You’ve summited above 6,000m, on our Mera Peak trip or elsewhere
  • You’re an expert ski mountaineer, at home on steep, firm and variable snow with a pack
  • You can give the mountain a month
  • You’re comfortable with cold, tents and waiting for a weather window

It’s not for you if…

  • You haven’t been above 6,000m — start with Mera Peak
  • You need a fixed timetable — the weather sets the summit day
  • You want comfort — this is the opposite of the Annapurna Ski Expedition

Before You Book

What you’ll need.

Qualification

A summit above 6,000m, on our Mera Peak Ski Expedition or elsewhere. We’ll ask for the details before you pay a deposit.

Ski level

Expert ski mountaineer: steep, firm and variable snow, with a pack, at altitude, after a long day.

Fitness & time

Very high endurance fitness and about 30 days away. We send a training plan and a full kit list when your place is confirmed.

Summit & descent

The summit and the ski descent depend on weather, snow and how the group is acclimatising. The guides make every call, and some years the right call is to turn round.

Questions

Asked, and answered honestly.

Why do I need a 6,000m summit first?

Because 7,126m is a big step, and how you handle 6,000m is the best guide to how you’ll handle 7,000m. Mera Peak is the natural way to qualify, and we’re happy to look at other summits case by case.

Is oxygen used?

The expedition is planned without supplementary oxygen for climbing. Emergency oxygen and a full medical kit are kept at base camp.

How much skiing is there?

You’ll ski the lower slopes during the acclimatisation rotations, and the summit descent from as high as conditions allow. Snow on a 7,000m peak varies a lot from year to year, and the guides decide what’s skiable.

Can I do Mera and Himlung in the same year?

Yes. Mera in April 2028 and Himlung in the autumn is an ideal progression, and it gives you a summer to train.

Does this help with an Everest plan?

Nepal is moving to require a 7,000m Nepali summit before an Everest permit. Himlung, at 7,126m in Nepal, would meet that.
