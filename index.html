Safety

The mountain sets the plan.
The guides make the call.

Skiing at altitude in Nepal is a serious undertaking on every trip we run, from 4,130m to 7,126m. We don’t minimise that. These rules apply to all of them.

The guides decide

Every ski day, route and summit attempt is signed off by HST’s guide team, based on snowpack, weather and the group on the day. When they say stop, we stop.

Altitude, taken slowly

Every itinerary climbs in stages with acclimatisation nights and daily health checks, and the big peaks have spare days built in for weather.

Ready for the worst

Avalanche kit is mandatory, helicopter evacuation is arranged before anyone goes high, and insurance that covers it is a condition of booking.
